Selected work

dot.o Launcher · Android home screen, Google Play

2026 – present

Sole designer and developer · case study at dbdoo.dev/work/doto-launcher

  • Designed a home screen with no grid, where apps and widgets sit anywhere on one canvas, and took it from concept to public release on Google Play.
  • Ran a beta and turned tester feedback into shipped design changes: light themes for daylight use, controls moved within thumb reach, and a way to hold the screen still that one tester asked for three times.
  • Replaced a seven-step onboarding tutorial with one-time hints that appear only when a gesture would help and disappear once used.
  • Audited the appearance settings (15 overlapping controls, 6 names for one concept) and restructured them into complete looks plus six clearly named parts.
  • Designed for accessibility: menus follow the phone's text size, touch targets are generous, and theme changes never override a user's motion settings.
