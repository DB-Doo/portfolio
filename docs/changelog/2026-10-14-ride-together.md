# The Roomba rides down with the spider (2026-10-14)

Before: only the spider went down its line as you scrolled; the Roomba stayed on the floor.

After: once the spider is hanging, the Roomba joins it, a different way each time:

- **Lasso:** the spider casts a thread down to the Roomba and reels it up to hang underneath.
- **Jet:** the Roomba lifts off on little puffs, flies to the line and slides down it to just above the spider.
- **Hop:** it crouches, springs onto the line in one arc and slides down.

Wakes first (later the same day): before, a Roomba asleep on its charger shot up to the line still sleepy-eyed. After, its eyes open with a blink of its ring, it rolls out of the dock, and only then sets off to join the spider, awake for the whole ride. The three ways are also more distinct (the jet climbs on bigger puffs, the hop tumbles and bounces the line, the lasso tugs), and both critters turn smoothly instead of shimmering while they hang.

While riding it now and then turns slowly on its thread. Back at the top they both step off where they hang.

Capture (laptop and 6 Pro): three slow scrolls from the top past the first screen, so all three ways show up (on a phone, without touching the screen first, so the Roomba starts asleep in its dock), and one scroll back to the top.

## What it gets up to on the way down

Before: once on the line, the Roomba just rode along. After, it has eight things it does while you read:

- **Progress ring:** its light ring fills as you go down the page and flashes green at the bottom.
- **Dozing:** stop reading for a while and it nods off, breathing slowly with its ring dimmed. The next scroll jolts it awake, wide-eyed.
- **Bungee:** a hard flick drops it on a stretchy cord. It bounces back while its side brush spins like a propeller to steady it.
- **Victory lap:** at the bottom it hops off the line onto the page for a lap, and rejoins when you scroll back up.
- **High fives:** it bumps the spider as each heading passes the middle of the window, with a little burst of sparks.
- **Pendulum:** stop after a quick scroll and the line swings, then settles.
- **Headlight:** it sweeps a soft beam over each card and picture it passes, and the dots part where the light lands.
- **Swapping places:** now and then it climbs round the spider to ride the other end, and the spider leans aside.

Capture (laptop and 6 Pro): one slow read from top to bottom, with a ten-second pause partway, one hard flick, the bottom, and back to the top.
