# One sheet: the site becomes a Doto fabric (2026-10-13)

Before: dbdoo.dev was a dark page with a dot field only in the hero and on the Doto page. The spider and Roomba lived in the home hero. Work had three cards: Doto, Wide and Bid Tracker.

After: one rubbery dot sheet runs behind every page and scrolls with it. Cards sit in wells the cloth gathers around and sink deeper on hover; text presses the dots lightly. Clicking a case-study card grows it into the case study while the sheet parts around it. The spider and Roomba roam every page, treating text as walls and cards and images as ledges. Bid Tracker is gone; NinKeys, the iPhone swipe keyboard, has its own case study.

## Doto's three standouts lead (2026-10-13, later)

Before: /doto-launcher listed music as one of four reasons, and the music and 3D shots sat in the screenshot strip.

After: an "Only on Doto" section right under the hero puts the music visualizer, the 3D canvas and Canvas Critters first, each in its own fabric well. The hero line, the home intro and the Doto card summary name all three.

## The spider abseils down the page (2026-10-13, later)

Before: the spider stayed in the window with the Roomba as you scrolled.

After: once you scroll past the first screen, the spider pays out a silk line from where it stands, swings into hanging head first, drifts to the right edge and lowers itself as you read, reaching the bottom of the window as you reach the bottom of the page. A sudden scroll swings it. Back at the top it steps off the line and carries on.

Capture: a slow scroll from the top to the bottom of the home page and back, on the laptop and the 6 Pro.
