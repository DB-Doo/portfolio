# One sheet: the site becomes a Doto fabric (2026-10-13)

Before: dbdoo.dev was a dark page with a dot field only in the hero and on the Doto page. The spider and Roomba lived in the home hero. Work had three cards: Doto, Wide and Bid Tracker.

After: one rubbery dot sheet runs behind every page and scrolls with it. Cards sit in wells the cloth gathers around and sink deeper on hover; text presses the dots lightly. Clicking a case-study card grows it into the case study while the sheet parts around it. The spider and Roomba roam every page, treating text as walls and cards and images as ledges. Bid Tracker is gone; NinKeys, the iPhone swipe keyboard, has its own case study.
